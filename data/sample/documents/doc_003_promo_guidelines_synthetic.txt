Promotion guidelines
Effective 2025-12-01, version 2.1

1. A promotion normally runs for 7 days, Monday to Sunday.
2. A percent discount should not be more than 25 percent without approval from
   the pricing manager.
3. Every promotion needs an ID, a product, a start date, an end date and the
   region and channel it applies to.
4. Competitor prices are logged with a date and a source. Prices without a
   source are not used in reports.
5. The same price applies to every customer in a region and channel. Prices are
   not set for individual customers.
6. Pricing staff review any answer from the copilot before acting on it.
