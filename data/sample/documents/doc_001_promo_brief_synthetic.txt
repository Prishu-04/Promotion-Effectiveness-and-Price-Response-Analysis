Promotion brief: PROMO-001, Orange Juice 1L
Effective 2026-01-12, version 1.0

Plan
We will run 10 percent off Orange Juice 1L (SunGrove) in North region stores
for the week of 2026-01-19 to 2026-01-25. The online channel is not part of
this promotion and keeps its regular price. No loyalty card is needed.

Reason
The category team wants to test a mid-sized discount on a high-volume item.
Shelf signs will show the discounted price.

Risks noted by the team
FreshMart was seen with a lower shelf price the week before the promotion
starts. Part of any sales change could come from shoppers switching back from
FreshMart, so the result should not be read as the discount alone.
